你的上色作品与偏好设置仅保存在设备本地。卸载应用即全部删除。我们无法访问，也无法为你恢复。

Chroma (the "App") is provided by an independent developer ("we" or "us"). We take your privacy seriously. The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

1. We Do Not Collect Any Data

The App runs entirely offline:

  • No data is transmitted over the network;
  • No registration or sign-in is required;
  • No personal information, device information, or usage data is collected;
  • No ad SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking components are included;
  • Your location, contacts, and photo library are never read. The "Add to Photo Library" permission is requested only when you export artwork, and is write-only.

2. Local Storage

Your coloring works and preferences are stored on your device only. Uninstalling the App deletes everything. We have no access to it and cannot recover it for you.

3. In-App Purchase

The App offers a one-time, non-consumable purchase to unlock all line-art templates. Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store; we never receive your payment information. You can restore a previous purchase via "Restore Purchases" in the App.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for children. Because no data is collected at all, no children's personal information is involved (in line with COPPA, GDPR-K, and the Personal Information Protection Law of the PRC).

5. Third-Party Services

The App only uses system services provided by Apple: App Store in-app purchases, and Apple's anonymous aggregated analytics (which do not allow us to identify any individual).

6. Changes to This Policy

If future features ever involve data collection, we will update this policy and its date on this page.
