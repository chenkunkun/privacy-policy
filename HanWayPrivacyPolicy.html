HanWay汉途 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

HanWay汉途 ("the App", "we") is a paid, offline Chinese-learning app.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. No account

The App has no sign-up and no user profile.

2. No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The App contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and makes no network requests.

3. Your content stays on your device

Learning progress, saved words, quiz results and recordings are stored locally on your device (SwiftData/local storage). They are never uploaded.

4. Microphone

Used only when you choose to record your pronunciation for self-comparison. Recordings are temporary files in the App's own storage and are never transmitted.

5. Speech recognition

Pronunciation scoring uses Apple's on-device speech recognition (requiresOnDeviceRecognition). Audio is processed entirely on your device. When a device cannot run on-device recognition, scoring is disabled rather than sending audio anywhere.

6. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details. The App uses no purchase analytics.

7. Children

The App contains no age-restricted content and collects no data from anyone, including children.
